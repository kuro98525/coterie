The premise

You don’t need more connections. You need the right three.

The modern job market is a shouting match. Algorithms reward volume, recruiters cast wider nets, and the roles worth having are rarely advertised at all.

Coterie is built the other way around: a small, reviewed membership, a human curator for every search, and an introduction only when there is a real reason.

How it works

Four conversations. No black boxes.

Our search desk works like a private office, not a marketplace. You’ll always know who is handling your search, what they’ve been told, and what happens next.

From the desk This week
  • Shortlist deliveredChief of Staff, London
  • Mandate signedPrivate markets, Singapore
  • Introduction acceptedDesign × Luxury
  1. 01

    A proper conversation.

    Thirty minutes with a curator, not a form. What you want, what you’d refuse, and what you’re ready for next.

    30 minutes · One named curator
  2. 02

    A brief worth reading.

    One page on you, written by a human and approved by you. No keyword soup, no visibility without consent.

    48 hours · Approved in writing
  3. 03

    Three introductions.

    A short list, not a feed. Each comes with why it fits and who you’ll meet. Both sides opt in before a call is booked.

    9 days · Never more than three
  4. 04

    The quiet part.

    Terms, references, notice periods. We stay through the decision — and for the first ninety days after it.

    Through offer & onboarding
